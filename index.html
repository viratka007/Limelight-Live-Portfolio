OUR STORY

Who We Are

Limelight is the Drama Club of IIIT Dharwad — a student collective united by theatre, storytelling, and creative expression. We believe creativity belongs alongside technical learning, giving students a stage to explore ideas, discover their voices, and bring stories to life.

MORE THAN ENTERTAINMENT

Stories that stay with you

From satire and humour to socially relevant narratives, our performances aim to do more than entertain. Through acting, direction, scriptwriting, and stagecraft, we create experiences that invite audiences to reflect, connect, and see the world from another perspective.
